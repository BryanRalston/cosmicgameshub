FAQ

If you care about contrast and motion, 240Hz 1440p QD-OLED around $350 is the first time we would call OLED a value upgrade. If you already smash 400 FPS in CS2 on a bright desk, IPS or 480Hz OLED is the more honest spend.
Risk is lower than 2020 TVs, not zero. Taskbars and static minimaps are the issue. Use pixel care, hide the taskbar, read the warranty.
QD-OLED usually wins color volume. WOLED implementations vary with coating. Buy the price and the firmware, not the acronym.
