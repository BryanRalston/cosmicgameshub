🎮 Look, then place

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and right-drag to pan. The first brick is a 2×2.

  • Place: Pick a brick, then click the plate or another brick
  • Rotate: Press R before you click
  • Color: Choose a swatch. Bricks snap to the stud grid
Tap a starter to drop a build, or close this and place a brick.

🛠️ Change the build

  • Delete (X) and Undo (Ctrl+Z)
  • Paint (P), Face paint (Shift+P), Dropper (I)
  • Wall and Floor drag out a run of bricks
  • Mirror (M) and Transparent (T)

📦 Walk, save, share

  • Walk (V): WASD, mouse to look, Space to jump
  • Save: Project save, or Gallery, keeps the build in this browser
  • Slots 1–5 and ? for the rest of the keys
Have fun building. There is no wrong way to stack.
