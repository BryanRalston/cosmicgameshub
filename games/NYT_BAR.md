# NYT bar — flagship dailies

Editorial law for Pixle, the Mini crossword, Link, and Gamerdle.
Not for BRIX, Beat Lab, Asteroid, Void, Aim. Those are a different sport.

## What this bar is
- Same puzzle for everyone that **UTC** day
- **Constructed**, not RNG from a junk list
- You understand in five seconds; help is a **?** modal, not a tutorial overlay
- Play surface is type + grid. Almost no decoration. Thumb-first
- Share grid is the marketing (emoji / clean 1080 card)
- Colorblind-friendly states (marks, not color-only)
- Stats: played, win%, streak, and a distribution where it fits
- Zero broken days. A dud puzzle is a ship-blocker

## What this bar is not
- More nebula art on the board
- A 25th Wordle clone
- Auto-generated Connections groups
- Clues that read "gaming term for X"

## Pixle
- Curated **answer** list only: common 5-letter gaming words a player will nod at
- Separate **allowed-guess** dictionary (thousands of 5-letter English words)
- GQZXV is "not in list"; CRANE is accepted even if it is not today's answer
- Guess distribution 1–6 in the stats modal
- Hard mode optional (revealed greens stay, yellows must be reused)
- Colorblind patterns on tiles **and** keys
- No emoji CTA grid on the play surface; Play next lives under the result modal

## Mini crossword
- Fully checked 5×5 (five across, five down, all real words)
- Clues have a voice (witty / gaming). Never "gaming term for X"
- Drop any grid that is not fully checked
- Sticky clue for the active cell on mobile
- Share is filled time + a fair grid

## Link (`/games/link`)
- Cosmic name. Do not call it Connections or NYT
- Four groups of four, hand-written
- Yellow / green / blue / purple = easy → expert
- Shuffle tiles. Four mistakes. Share the solve **order**, not the words
- Categories a gamer would argue about
- If you cannot hand-write an honest set, do not ship that day
- Never RNG a group from a tag list

## Gamerdle
- Daily **target** is a curated household-name list
- Guess bank can stay wide (autocomplete commit stays)
- Weekdays are not obscure shovelware
- Share grid of attributes stays clean; colorblind marks on cells

## RIFT Decode
- Gaming terms only. Pasta / clouds / rainbow stay quarantined
- Bank large enough that it does not loop every two weeks

## Desk
- `/games/daily` is a games desk, not an arcade banner
- Twelve slots. Link replaced Word Scramble on the desk; scramble remains an anytime on `/games`
