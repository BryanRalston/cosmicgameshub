    How Orbit works

    Orbit is a free daily word puzzle from CosmicGamesHub. Eight spokes run out from a glowing core through four rings of letters. Read each spoke from the core outward: when all eight spell common English words, the orbit locks and you win.

    The inner ring is fixed. Turn the other three rings by dragging them, with the turn buttons, or with your arrow keys. A spoke glows gold the moment it spells a word. Each puzzle has exactly one solution, checked against every possible ring position. Par is the fewest turns that solve it; stay within two and a half times par for three stars.

    Puzzles get harder through the week, from a gentle Monday to a tough Saturday. Stuck? A ring check shows which rings are already in place, and a ring lock snaps one into place.

    How to play

    • 🪐
      Turn the rings until every spoke, read from the core outward, spells a 4-letter word.
    • 👆
      Drag a ring, or pick a ring and tap the turn buttons. Keys: ↑↓ pick, ←→ turn, Z undo.
    • ✨
      A spoke glows gold when it is a word. Light all eight to lock the orbit. The inner ring never moves.
    • ⭐
      Every 45° step a ring passes is one move, including drags and undo. Par is the fewest moves; stay within 2.5× par for 3 stars. Many spokes spell a word that isn't the answer, so check the others.
    • 💡
      Stuck? Hint offers a ring check (which rings are already in place; 3 stars still possible within 2× par) and a ring lock (max 2 stars), once each a day.
    • 📅
      Puzzles ramp up through the week: gentlest on Monday, toughest on Saturday.
