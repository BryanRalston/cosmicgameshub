Superlight 2 vs G PRO X2 SUPERSTRIKE

SUPERSTRIKE is not a new shape. It is Superlight 2 with the Haptic Inductive Trigger System on the main buttons: electromagnetic travel sensing, haptic feedback, adjustable actuation, rapid-trigger-style reset. August 2026 CS2 tracking still has Superlight 2 first and SUPERSTRIKE inside the top five. Pay the extra money for the click, or do not buy it.

Quick Verdict
Default Logitech shell → Superlight 2 · Want analog clicks → SUPERSTRIKE
Same hand feel. Superlight 2 is cheaper, lighter by a gram, and still the CS2 volume leader. SUPERSTRIKE is the interesting 2026 Logitech mouse. HITS takes a session to learn. Public notes include possible left/right click variance. That is a reason to be picky, not a reason to panic.

Head-to-Head

Shape and weight
Essentially tied
Both measure 125 x 63.5 x 40mm. Superlight 2 is 60g. SUPERSTRIKE is 61g. If Superlight 2 already fits, SUPERSTRIKE will fit. If Superlight 2 never fit, buy a Viper or DeathAdder instead of a second Logitech shell.
Clicks
SUPERSTRIKE is the product
LIGHTFORCE hybrids on Superlight 2 are known, stable, and optical-adjacent. HITS on SUPERSTRIKE tracks button travel and fires a haptic. Actuation and reset are tunable. That is Rapid Trigger logic on a mouse. It is also new enough that some reviewers mention a learning curve and possible channel imbalance. CosmicGamesHub did not measure 30ms click claims in a lab.
Battery and extras
Superlight 2
Logitech cites about 100 hours at 1 kHz on Superlight 2 versus about 90 hours on SUPERSTRIKE because HITS draws more. Same HERO 2, same 8 kHz wireless. POWERPLAY 2 support is a SUPERSTRIKE talking point. Scroll encoder differs (TTC blue on SUPERSTRIKE per TechPowerUp).
Price
Superlight 2
Superlight 2 often streets near $114. SUPERSTRIKE is $180. That gap only makes sense if analog clicks are the feature you were missing. Otherwise Superlight 2, Viper V3 Pro, or G305 X Superlight is the saner receipt.
