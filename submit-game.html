For game makers

Get your game on Cosmic Games Hub

Made a free browser game? Send it our way. We play every submission, and the ones we love get listed with credit and a link back to you.

Submit your game →

Opens a short Google Form in a new tab. No sign-in needed.

Why submit

  • Reach players who come for quick, free browser games.
  • Be considered for Game of the Week, the big feature slot on our homepage.
  • Every listing credits you and links back to your game.

What we look for

  • Free to play in the browser, no download.
  • Family-friendly content.
  • Works on phone and desktop.
  • Loads fast and is fun in the first minute.

How it works

  1. Submit the form.
  2. We play your game.
  3. If we list it, we reply by email, usually within a week.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Submitting and being listed on Cosmic Games Hub costs nothing.

Can you embed my game?

Only with your permission. The form asks, and we only embed a game if you say yes.

What gets featured?

Game of the Week is a rotating slot on our homepage. Any submitted game can be considered for it.

Will every game be listed?

Not necessarily. We only reply if we list your game.
